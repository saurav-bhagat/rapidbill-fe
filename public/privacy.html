Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

RapidBill is billing and stock software for small businesses in India, available as a website and a mobile app. This page explains what we keep, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short on purpose.

What we collect

  • Your account: your name, email address and a password (stored only in scrambled form, never in plain text).
  • Your business records: what you enter to run your business, such as business details, items, stock, customers and suppliers, and bills. This is your data. We keep it so the app can work for you and sync between your devices.
  • Payments for your plan: if you buy a plan, the payment is handled by our payment provider. We receive the result of the payment and keep a record and invoice for it. We never see or store your card or UPI details.
  • Basic technical information that any online service needs to run safely, such as request logs used to keep the service secure and fix faults.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not use ad or tracking tools in the app.
  • We do not read your business records except when you ask us for help or the law requires it.

How we use it

To run your account, keep your records and sync them across your devices, email you sign-in recovery codes and service messages, process payments for your plan, keep the service safe, and give you support.

Device permissions

  • Camera (optional): only to scan barcodes while you bill. Images are not saved or sent.
  • Face ID / fingerprint (optional): to unlock the app on your phone. This is handled by your phone; we never receive your biometric data.
  • Bluetooth (optional): only to connect to your receipt printer.

Who helps us run RapidBill

We use trusted service providers for cloud hosting, payments and sending email. They handle data only to provide those services to us, and only what each service needs. We may also share information when the law requires it.

Keeping your data safe

Data is sent over encrypted connections, passwords are stored in scrambled form, and each business’s records are kept separate from other businesses. No online service can promise perfect security, so please use a strong password and keep your devices locked.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. You can delete your account yourself in the app or on the website under Settings. Deleting removes your account and your business records. We keep only what the law requires us to keep, such as records of payments and the invoices we issued to you.

Your choices

You can see and change your details in the app, export your records, and delete your account at any time. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by contacting us below.

Children

RapidBill is made for business use and is not meant for children.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app when needed.
