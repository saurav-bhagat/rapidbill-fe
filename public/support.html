Common questions

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap Forgot password. We email a 6-digit code to your email address. Enter it and choose a new password. If no email arrives, check spam, then contact us.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can keep billing offline; everything saves on your device and syncs automatically when you are back online.

Can I run more than one business?

Yes. Add another business from the business switcher at the top of the app. Each business keeps its own bills, items and customers.

My trial or plan has ended

You can still open the app and see all your records. To create new bills again, choose a plan under Plans in the app or on the website.

How do I get my data out?

Use the export and backup options in Settings. Contact us if you would like help.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, scroll to the bottom and choose Delete account. This removes your account and your business records. You can do this in the app or on the website. Contact us if you need help.
